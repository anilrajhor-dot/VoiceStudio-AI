Privacy

Projects, voice samples and settings are stored locally in your browser (localStorage/IndexedDB) unless you connect cloud storage. Nothing is uploaded unless you connect a backend/API.

Terms of Use

Only clone or synthesize a voice you have explicit permission to use. Do not use character voices, dubbing or generated speech to impersonate a real person or to mislead an audience. Generated music and voices carry the license terms of the connected provider — this app does not itself grant commercial or royalty-free rights.
